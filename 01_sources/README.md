# 01_sources/

Research behind the personas and protocol:

- `cvpr_official_process.md`: official CVPR review form, guidelines, policies and timelines
  (primary sources quoted with URLs).
- `multi_venue_review_design.md`: versioned ICLR/ICML/NeurIPS sources, curated personas, the
  careful-reading protocol and evidence limitations.
- `real_review_patterns.md`: patterns measured in 184 public OpenReview reviews of computer-vision
  papers (ICLR 2024-2026, NeurIPS 2024-2025): weakness frequencies, lengths, reviewer types,
  rebuttal dynamics. Quotes are short excerpts; papers are cited by codes P01-P46.
- `reviewer_behavior_literature.md`: studies of reviewer behaviour, LLM reviewers and
  reward hacking, with design rules derived from them.
- `figure_research.md`: evidence behind the figure review (graphical perception, colour vision,
  venue figure guidance, figure complaints in the review corpus, how reliably vision-language
  models read figures), with the design rules and the files that implement them.
- `writing_feedback_research.md`: evidence behind the revision coach and the writing playbook
  (feedback science, constructive vs destructive peer review, scientific writing structure, CV/ML
  paper-writing advice, what LLM-generated feedback is good for and where it fails), with the
  design rules and the files that implement them.

The raw review downloads used for the counts are not redistributed. They come from public
OpenReview data and its HuggingFace mirrors named in `real_review_patterns.md`.
