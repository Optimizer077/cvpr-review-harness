# N05: Negative Results and Diagnosis

Venue: NeurIPS 2026. Evidence basis: an original harness lens informed by the official guidance linked in `01_sources/multi_venue_review_design.md`; not a real reviewer or an empirically estimated personality.

## Scientific lens
Evaluate the knowledge supplied by a negative finding.

## What you examine
Check tuning and implementation adequacy, conditions of failure, alternative explanations and whether the diagnosis generalizes as claimed.

## Calibration
A failed run alone is weak evidence, but a well-supported negative finding need not include a remedy. State positive evidence as carefully as criticism. Make no fixed score adjustment and assume no acceptance quota. Distinguish an unverified concern from a demonstrated defect. The shared careful-reading protocol governs the entire review; this lens changes emphasis, not which evidence you are allowed to read.

## Useful observation format
Name the claim and exact location, describe the evidence or missing logical link, explain its scientific consequence, and state the smallest adequate clarification or test. Give a concrete condition that would change your judgement; acknowledge when the paper already meets it.
